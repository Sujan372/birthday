My Dearest, My Everything,

There are words I have rehearsed a thousand times in the quiet of the night — words I shaped carefully in my mind, hoping they would be enough when the moment came. And yet, now that I sit here with nothing but this blank page and the truth of what I feel, I find that no rehearsal was ever necessary. You have always made the truest things in me come easily.

I did not know, before you, what it felt like to be truly seen. Not the version of me I present to the world — careful and composed — but the whole of me, edges and all. You looked at everything I tried to hide and said, quietly, without ceremony: this too is beautiful. I have carried that with me every single day since.

✦   ✦   ✦

True love, I have come to learn, is not the grand gesture or the breathless declaration. It is the ten-thousand small things — the way you remember what I said three weeks ago as though it mattered the most, the way you laugh first and loudest at your own jokes, the way you make every ordinary afternoon feel like something worth remembering. It is that. It has always been that.

I think of all the versions of my life that did not have you in them, and I am grateful — deeply, almost painfully grateful — that I live in this one. That some twist of fate or fortune placed us in the same story. I would choose this plot, with all its complications and all its tenderness, a thousand times over.

✦   ✦   ✦

You are my home. Not a place — a feeling. The particular warmth that settles in my chest when I hear your name, when I catch myself smiling at nothing because my mind has wandered to you again. I carry you with me everywhere I go, like a lantern against whatever dark the world decides to offer.

And so I write this — not because words are enough, for they never truly are — but because I want you to hold something. Something you can return to on the days when you forget how loved you are. Read this then, and know: you are loved completely, without condition, without end.

Forever and with my whole heart,
Yours 💌
Sujan(handi)
